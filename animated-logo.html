📦 Converting to GIF

  1. Option 1 - Online Converter:
    Use a tool like ezgif.com or svg2gif.com
    Upload the logo.svg file and convert to GIF
  2. Option 2 - Screen Recording:
    Record this animation with a screen recorder and convert to GIF
  3. Option 3 - Browser Developer Tools:
    Open browser DevTools → Performance → Record the animation → Export

💡 Tip: For email profile pictures, keep the GIF under 200KB and use dimensions of 200x200px or smaller.
